## III. Conclusion

This paper presents Decathlon VOC Analyzer, an evidence-driven multimodal VOC analysis system for aligning product image-text evidence with user reviews. The system standardizes raw product data into traceable evidence packages, extracts user reviews into aspect-level VOC objects, and then generates structured product analysis results through question planning, multimodal recall, reranking, report generation, and claim-level attribution.

The central conclusion of this paper is that product VOC analysis should not be reduced to one-step review summarization. Instead, it should be organized as a multi-stage process of "review aspect modeling - evidence question planning - product image-text retrieval - evidence-constrained generation." The current implementation has formed a runnable research prototype and remains consistent in structured intermediate representations, evidence attribution, replay mechanisms, and evaluation interfaces. Test results show that the current implementation passes 166 automated tests, indicating engineering-level stability in the main system chain and key modules.

Future work will focus on adding manually annotated benchmarks across multiple categories, systematically comparing different retrieval and reranking strategies, introducing finer-grained visual grounding, and further closing the loop between human feedback and the replay mechanism.
