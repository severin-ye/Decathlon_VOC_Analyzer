## Abstract

E-commerce voice-of-customer (VOC) analysis needs to understand both the subjective experience expressed in reviews and the objective evidence available on product pages. This paper presents Decathlon VOC Analyzer, an evidence-driven multimodal VOC analysis system. The method standardizes product information into evidence packages, extracts aspect-level signals from reviews, plans each aspect as a question with an explicit retrieval route, performs two-stage multimodal recall and reranking, and finally generates structured reports with claim-level evidence attribution. The system preserves key intermediate representations to support reproducibility and error analysis. The contributions of this paper include: a "review aspect modeling - question planning - multimodal retrieval - evidence-constrained generation" framework; a reproducible system based on structured intermediate representations; and system stability validated by 166 automated tests. This paper is positioned as a systems-method paper and does not claim to have completed a large-scale manually annotated benchmark.

## Keywords

multimodal VOC analysis; evidence-constrained generation; image-text retrieval; product review mining; claim attribution
